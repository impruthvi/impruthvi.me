## Priority Issues

**[P0] Availability signal is invisible — EM can't confirm active job search**
`SITE.availability = "Open to full-time roles"` is defined in `lib/constants.ts` but rendered nowhere on the homepage. An EM 80% convinced to reach out may not send the email if they assume the portfolio belongs to someone not in an active search. This is not a polish issue — it directly affects contact conversion.
Fix: Add a small availability badge in `components/hero.tsx`, below the "Laravel & Node.js Engineer" subtitle line. Use a muted, small treatment to preserve the One Red budget: `<span className="mt-2 inline-flex items-center gap-1.5 rounded-full border border-border/50 px-3 py-1 text-xs text-muted-foreground">{SITE.availability} · {SITE.location}</span>`. Pulls directly from constants so it stays in sync.
*`/impeccable harden components/hero.tsx`*

**[P1] MetricCard 4 ("PR Merged") breaks the grid and undersells the credential**
The first three cards display quantitative values in large mono bold Laravel Red: "4+", "50,000+", "$500K+". The fourth displays "PR Merged" — a text phrase where a number is expected. A PR merged into the Laravel core by its creator is the rarest credential on the page; the numeric card format makes it look like an afterthought alongside three business metrics.
Fix (preferred): Change value to `"1"`, label to `"PR in Laravel Core"`, subtext to `"Merged by Taylor Otwell"` in `lib/constants.ts`. Numeric, fits the grid, doesn't undersell. Alternative: remove from metric grid; the hero body copy and open source badge-pill already surface this credential correctly — don't need a third instance if the format is wrong.
*`/impeccable clarify lib/constants.ts`*

**[P1] CTA banner copy ends the page below its own standard**
"Let's build something great together" violates PRODUCT.md principle 4 ("no hustle-speak, no vague superlatives") and is the softest phrase the EM reads before the contact decision. The page opened with "PR merged by Taylor Otwell into the Laravel framework" and closes with a phrase indistinguishable from a Fiverr listing.
Fix: Replace with outcome-led copy that adds information rather than sentiment. Candidate: "Available for full-time roles in Ahmedabad and remote. Drop a line — I respond within 24 hours." Or: "Have a hard backend problem? Let's see if it's a fit." Either version restores the direct, specific voice and gives the EM a concrete expectation.
*`/impeccable clarify components/cta-banner.tsx`*

**[P2] One Red Rule: three confirmed violation sites — structurally impossible to comply without code changes**
Hero: 5 instances at rest (subtitle + 4 metric values). Experience: each job entry with 4 highlights emits 5 simultaneous `text-laravel` instances (1 dot + 4 bullet markers) — the section is unconditionally non-compliant with real data. Open Source: 6 instances across 3 items (icon + badge per item).
Fix hierarchy: (1) Experience — change bullet marker `<span className="mr-2 text-laravel">&bull;</span>` to `<span className="mr-2 text-muted-foreground">&bull;</span>` in `components/experience.tsx`. Timeline dot stays Laravel Red; bullet markers revert to muted. Net per entry: 1 dot — compliant. (2) Hero — change metric card value color from `text-laravel` to `text-foreground` in `components/metric-card.tsx`. The subtitle "Laravel & Node.js Engineer" retains Laravel Red; the four metric values use foreground. JetBrains Mono weight already differentiates them. Net hero: 1 instance — compliant. (3) Open Source — the section will read ≤3 instances if icons or badge pills, not both simultaneously, use `text-laravel`. Icon can stay; badge pill text can switch to `text-foreground` with `bg-laravel/10` border preserved.
*`/impeccable colorize`*

**[P2] Six case study cards present with no priority signal**
An EM with 90 seconds cannot process six equal-weight cards. No visual distinction between featured and supporting work. The two cards visible above the fold on desktop receive the clicks; the bottom four are invisible to the scanner.
Fix: Add `featured: true` to the top 2 case studies in their MDX frontmatter. In `app/page.tsx`, render featured studies with a distinct visual treatment (full-width or larger card) and show remaining 4 in a collapsible "More work →" link. Alternatively, change the grid to a prioritized list: first case study full-width, next two at half-width, rest behind "See all work."

Social icon tap targets (20px, no padding) sit adjacent to 32px button targets — thumb-tap collision risk when reaching for "Let's Talk." Primary CTAs and social icons need vertical separation on mobile.

**The Hiring Manager (Series B, ≤90s):** Opens → hero lands (strong) → metrics (strong, with one format oddity on card 4) → case studies (which two should I read? no signal) → experience (first bullets only, numbers are mid-sentence) → open source (second trust spike — good) → time up. CTA banner never seen. The page front-loads trust correctly but never re-escalates to give the EM a second reason to click at the decision moment.
